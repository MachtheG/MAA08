# Wajir photographs — credits

Real photographs of Wajir County from Wikimedia Commons. Each is licensed
CC BY-SA, so the credit below must stay visible on the site (the footer
credit line renders it). They were cropped to remove burned-in watermarks and
printed as palette duotones with `scripts/duotone.py land`; as adaptations of
CC BY-SA works, these files are themselves shared under the same licence.

They show the county, not the campaign: never present them as campaign events.
